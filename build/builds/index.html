Builds

Build patterns that avoid common launch-week mistakes

Most early Mistfall Hunter builds fail because they stack damage without solving escape timing, role overlap, or resource pressure.

Safe Extractor

Best for: Solo, early game, cautious players

Priorities: Mobility, sustain, escape utility

Avoid: Skipping exit tools for damage

Balanced Raider

Best for: Most mixed groups

Priorities: Reliable weapon, one defensive layer, flexible utility

Avoid: Duplicating the same team role

Boss Breaker

Best for: Planned boss pressure

Priorities: Burst window, uptime, recovery option

Avoid: Entering without a reset route

Scout Caller

Best for: Teams that need information

Priorities: Vision, mobility, low-noise disengage

Avoid: Fighting every contact
